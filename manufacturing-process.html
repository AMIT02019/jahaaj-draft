Packaging Capabilities

Packaging That Protects. Packaging That Performs.

Your packaging is the first touchpoint of your brand. At Jahaaj Healthcare, we combine functionality, protection, compliance, and visual appeal to create packaging that is ready for both shelf and shipment.

Primary Packaging

Direct contact formats designed to preserve bio-active efficacy:

  • ✔ Pouches & Foil Packs
  • ✔ Bottles & Jars (HDPE, PET, Amber Glass)
  • ✔ Containers & Buckets
  • ✔ Single-Dose Sachets
  • ✔ Blister & Strip Packs
  • ✔ Jerry Cans & Industrial Drums
  • ✔ Tablet & Bolus Containers

Secondary Packaging

Outer presentation and transit-ready protection:

  • ✔ Printed Folding Cartons & Outer Boxes
  • ✔ Corrugated & Master Cartons (Heavy Duty 5-ply / 7-ply)
  • ✔ Protective Shrink Wrapping
  • ✔ Export-Ready Palletization & Strapping
