12-Step Manufacturing Process

From initial concept and formulation R&D to certified testing, packaging, and dispatch — our end-to-end manufacturing process ensures consistent quality, compliance, and complete batch traceability.

STEP 01

Product Idea Discussion & Briefing

Collaborative discussion on product vision, target animal species, nutritional goals, dosage formats, and commercial expectations.

STEP 02

Customized Product Development

In-house veterinary R&D team (40+ years experience) formulates bespoke, synergistic, and bioavailable active recipes.

STEP 03

Sample Manufacturing

Precision pilot batch preparation of lab samples to test physical appearance, taste/palatability, and mixing characteristics.

STEP 04

Laboratory Testing

Rigorous active ingredient assays, physical parameters, stability assessments, and microbiological screening.

STEP 05

Sample Approval from Client

Formal client evaluation, sensory sign-off, and final specification lock-in prior to initiating packaging and sourcing.

STEP 06

Artwork & Label Finalization

Label artwork adaptation, regulatory compliance checks with domestic and export feed standards, and printing approval.

STEP 07

Procurement of Packaging Materials

Sourcing high-barrier pouches, jars, HDPE containers, blister foils, and carton master shippers engineered for farm stability.

STEP 08

Raw Materials Sourcing

Procuring validated feed-grade actives, vitamins, and minerals with supplier CoA verification and incoming quarantine testing.

STEP 09

Commercial-Scale Production

Cleanroom blending, rotary bolus compression, or SS-316 liquid manufacturing adhering to strict FAMI-QS & HACCP SOPs.

STEP 10

Quality Inspection of Finished Product

Comprehensive QA inspection including assay potency, weight variation, dissolution, and pathogen screening for release.

STEP 11

Final Product Approval by Customer

Customer review and verification of batch testing results and Certificate of Analysis (CoA) prior to dispatch.

STEP 12

Dispatch of Finished Goods

Palletized, shrink-wrapped, and barcoded export-ready shipment dispatched through tracked logistics to your warehouse.
