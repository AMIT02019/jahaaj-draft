The 12 Steps of Our Manufacturing Process

Each phase is executed under FAMI-QS certified quality management, rigorous R&D oversight, and full batch traceability.

STEP 01 Phase 1: Discovery

Product Idea Discussion & Briefing

Initial technical consultation to define nutritional targets, animal species focus, dosage format, cost parameters, and timeline requirements.

STEP 02 Phase 1: Formulation

Customized Product Development

In-house R&D specialists formulate custom recipes using bio-available active ingredients, tailored for optimal palatability and stability.

STEP 03 Phase 2: Pilot Sampling

Sample Manufacturing

Production of bench-scale pilot samples to evaluate physical characteristics, powder flowability, solubility, and bulk density.

STEP 04 Phase 2: Laboratory QC

Laboratory Testing

Comprehensive analytical, chemical, and microbiological testing in our certified quality lab to verify active potency and purity.

STEP 05 Phase 2: Client Approval

Sample Approval from Client

Sample dispatch to client for sensory evaluation, field trials, and formal technical sign-off prior to commercial scale-up.

STEP 06 Phase 3: Design & Artwork

Artwork & Label Finalization

Collaborative packaging graphics design, regulatory label compliance verification, claims review, and final artwork sign-off.

STEP 07 Phase 3: Packaging Sourcing

Procurement of Packaging Materials

Procurement of approved sachets, pouches, bottles, jars, or bulk drums according to client specifications and barrier needs.

STEP 08 Phase 3: cGMP Sourcing

Raw Materials Sourcing

Sourcing high-purity active ingredients from audited, cGMP certified vendors with complete Certificate of Analysis (COA) verification.

STEP 09 Phase 4: Commercial Scale

Commercial-Scale Production

Full-scale automated manufacturing at our Rabale MIDC plant (1,200 TPA powders, 120 TPA boluses) under strict cGMP protocols.

STEP 10 Phase 4: Quality Inspection

Quality Inspection of Finished Product

Multi-point in-process checks and finished batch QA sampling against Certificate of Analysis (COA) safety benchmarks.

STEP 11 Phase 4: Customer Approval

Final Product Approval by Customer

Client review of batch inspection results and formal Certificate of Analysis (COA) approval prior to warehouse release.

STEP 12 Phase 4: Global Dispatch

Dispatch of Finished Goods

Protective palletization and dispatch from our Navi Mumbai hub via direct highway and port corridors across domestic & export markets.

Rabale MIDC Facility

Plant Capabilities & Specialized Equipment

Operating out of a 12,000 sq. ft. cGMP facility equipped with modern powder blending, compression, and analytical infrastructure.

Powder Blending & Premix (1,200 TPA)

High-capacity ribbon blenders and sifting units ensuring homogenous ingredient distribution across batch sizes.

Bolus & Tablet Compression (120 TPA)

Automated compression presses for veterinary boluses, tablets, and specialized chewable nutritional formats.

Chemical & Analytical Testing Lab

In-house quality control laboratory equipped for active assay verification, moisture analysis, and raw material purity testing.

QC Instrument & Precision Testing Room

Calibrated precision instruments ensuring audit-ready documentation and analytical accuracy for every production lot.

Blister & Strip Packaging Lines

Flexible packaging capabilities including blister packing, strip packing, sachets, jars, bottles, and bulk drums.

Air Conditioned Storage & Warehousing

Temperature-controlled raw material and finished goods warehousing maintaining ingredient stability and potency.
