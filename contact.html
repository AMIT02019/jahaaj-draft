Legal & Compliance

Privacy Policy & Terms of Use

Our commitments to proprietary formulation confidentiality, data privacy, and ethical contract manufacturing.

Privacy & Data Protection

At Jahaaj Healthcare Pvt. Ltd., we treat client commercial information, corporate correspondence, and contact records with the highest security. We do not sell, rent, or lease partner contact data to any third party.

  • Information collected via enquiry forms is used solely to evaluate CDMO feasibility and provide technical quotes.
  • All technical exchanges are stored on encrypted internal servers with role-based access control.
  • You may request deletion or export of your correspondence records at any time by contacting our compliance team.

Terms of Use & Mutual NDA

All formulation co-development and contract manufacturing relationships with Jahaaj Healthcare are governed by formal bilateral Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs).

  • Client IP Ownership: Custom formulation recipes, proprietary excipient ratios, and branded artwork remain 100% client intellectual property.
  • Non-Compete Assurance: We do not market or commercialize private-label formulations developed exclusively for partner brands.
  • Quality Compliance: Batch releases adhere strictly to mutually agreed Certificate of Analysis (CoA) specifications under FAMI-QS and ISO 9001 guidelines.
