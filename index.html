Product Journey

From Concept to Commercial Batch

Our streamlined 4-step CDMO process ensures technical precision, full IP confidentiality, and reliable delivery at every milestone.

01

Formulation Brief & Lab Pilot

Requirement definition, active titration, veterinary doctor formulation review, and bench-top trial batch formulation under strict bilateral NDA.

  • Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement
  • Actives synergy & cost modeling
  • 5 kg laboratory test batch
02

Analytical QC & Stability Trials

Physical-chemical testing, disintegration, moisture titration, and accelerated climate chamber stability testing to verify active potency retention.

  • HPLC & spectrophotometric assay
  • Accelerated shelf-life testing
  • Palatability & flowability audit
03

Cleanroom Blending & Packaging

Class 100,000 cleanroom blending in SS 316 double-cone mixers, automated sachet/pouch nitrogen flushing, and high-speed blister compression.

  • CV < 5% mixing homogeneity
  • Automated barrier packaging
  • Batch serialization & coding
04

Analytical Release & Logistics

Comprehensive Certificate of Analysis (CoA) generation, export dossier documentation, and rapid freight dispatch via JNPT Nhava Sheva.

  • 100% finished batch release
  • Full export dossier & FSC
  • 35 km to JNPT Ocean Terminal
World-Class Plant

Built for Precision, Scalability & Quality

Take a closer look inside our 12,000 sq. ft. purpose-built animal healthcare manufacturing facility at Rabale MIDC, Navi Mumbai.

Powder Blending

Cleanroom Powder Mixing Suite

Double-cone and ribbon blenders constructed with Stainless Steel 316 contact parts, positive-pressure air zoning, and micro-dosing systems ensuring complete homogeneity.

Bolus & Blister Line

High-Speed Rotary Compression

High-tonnage rotary presses producing up to 60,000 boluses daily with automated Alu-Alu and PVC moisture-barrier blister lines protecting sensitive actives.

Quality Assurance

Analytical QC & Chemistry Lab

Equipped with HPLC, UV-Vis spectrophotometer, Karl Fischer titrators, and analytical balances for complete raw material quarantine and batch release.

Microbiology

Microbiological Testing Suite

Laminar airflow hoods and pathogen incubators ensuring zero microbial or fungal contamination across probiotics, enzymes, and nutritional feeds.

Raw Materials

Climate-Controlled Ingredient Store

Segregated air-conditioned active ingredient storage maintaining strict temperature and humidity parameters to preserve thermo-labile vitamins and live probiotic strains.

Packaging

Secondary Packaging & Warehousing

Dedicated packaging lines for pouches, HDPE jars, fiber drums, and export carton palletization with batch coding and tamper-evident induction sealing.
