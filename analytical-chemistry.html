Dedicated Testing Suite

End-to-End Quantitative Chemical Verification

At our Rabale MIDC manufacturing facility, our analytical chemical laboratory serves as the frontline safeguard for raw material potency and finished product integrity. Every active ingredient is subjected to rigorous quantitative testing before entering the cleanroom production stream.

  • Active Ingredient Quantification: Validating guaranteed active concentrations across vitamins, electrolytes, enzymes, and phytogenics.
  • Trace Moisture Testing: Karl Fischer titration ensuring moisture thresholds are strictly controlled to prevent active degradation.
  • Organic Acid Purity: Measuring acid-insoluble ash, pH stability, and free organic acid fractions.
Climate-Controlled QC Instrument Room
Precision Instrumentation

Spectrophotometry & Physical Stability Testing

Housed in a strictly climate-controlled environment ($22^\circ\text{C} \pm 1^\circ\text{C}$ and $<45\%\text{ RH}$), our precision instruments verify physical parameters and active chemical bonds.

  • UV-Vis Spectrophotometry: Assessing absorption spectra and active vitamin kinetics over 24-month accelerated stability chambers.
  • Dissolution & Disintegration: Rotary tablet hardness, friability index (<0.5%), and effervescent dissolution speed (<180s).
  • Chelation Bonding Analysis: Verifying covalent bonding between minerals (Zn, Cu, Mn) and organic amino acid carriers.
Standard Operating Procedures

Standard Analytical Assays & Methodologies

All testing protocols adhere to international pharmacopeial (IP/BP/USP) and feed safety analytical standards.

HPLC Active Potency Assay

High-Performance Liquid Chromatography identifying and quantifying target actives with baseline separation and high linearity ($R^2 > 0.999$).

USP / In-House Validated

Karl Fischer Moisture Titration

Coulometric water determination for hygroscopic effervescent powders and water-soluble probiotics to prevent caking and potency decay.

Limit: < 2.0% - 3.5%

Heavy Metal Toxicity Screen

Atomic Absorption and ICP screening verifying Lead ($Pb$), Cadmium ($Cd$), Arsenic ($As$), and Mercury ($Hg$) remain far below regulatory maximum limits.

FAMI-QS Heavy Metal Limits

Ash & Acid Insoluble Ash

High-temperature muffle furnace incineration determining mineral purity and guaranteeing absence of silica or foreign adulterants.

ISO 5984 / AOAC Standards

Bulk Density & Particle Size

Tapped bulk density analysis and multi-sieve vibrating shaker classification ensuring uniform blending and zero demixing during transit.

CV < 5% Homogeneity

Formal Batch CoA Release

Every production batch receives a comprehensive Certificate of Analysis signed by certified analytical chemists prior to dispatch.
