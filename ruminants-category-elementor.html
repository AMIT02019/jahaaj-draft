Feed Conversion Ratio (FCR)

"Every Extra Kilogram of Feed Should Generate More Returns"

Feed represents 50-70% of the total cost of dairy production. When animals fail to convert feed efficiently, the result is higher feed costs, slower growth, lower milk production, and reduced returns. Better nutrient utilization allows every kilogram of feed to contribute more effectively towards growth, milk production, and overall herd performance.

Digestion & Rumen Health

"A Weak Rumen Doesn't Just Affect Digestion—It Affects Profitability"

The rumen is the powerhouse of every ruminant. Poor rumen function reduces fibre digestion, limits nutrient absorption, lowers feed efficiency, and increases the risk of metabolic disorders such as acidosis and ketosis. Supporting rumen health helps animals unlock more nutrients from every meal while maintaining long-term productivity.

Productivity

"Every Animal Has More Potential Than It May Be Showing"

When animals aren't performing to their genetic potential, farms lose profitability. Nutritional deficiencies, metabolic imbalances, environmental stress, and poor feed efficiency contribute to slower growth, reduced milk yield, and lower reproductive performance. Providing balanced nutritional support helps animals perform consistently throughout every stage.

Transition Cow Support

"The Most Critical Weeks Can Define the Entire Lactation"

The transition period around calving brings high metabolic demands that increase the risk of retained placenta, milk fever, ketosis, displaced abomasum, and reduced fertility. Supporting cows during this critical period promotes smoother recovery, healthier lactation, and improved reproductive performance.

Reproductive Performance

"Every Missed Conception Delays Your Profit"

Poor fertility is expensive. Each additional day that a cow remains open increases feeding, housing, and labour costs while reducing lifetime milk production. Balanced nutrition plays a vital role in improving conception rates, maintaining regular breeding cycles, and achieving healthier pregnancies.

Heat Stress Management

"Rising Temperatures Can Quietly Reduce Farm Income"

As temperatures rise, animals eat less, drink more, produce less milk, experience lower fertility, and become susceptible to disease. Research shows milk production can decline by 10-25% during heat stress. Providing nutritional support helps animals cope with environmental stress while maintaining productivity.

Udder Health & Mastitis

"One of the Costliest Diseases in Dairy Farming"

Mastitis is responsible for millions of dollars in losses worldwide every year from reduced milk yield, discarded milk, and treatment expenses. Supporting udder health helps maintain milk quality, strengthen natural defences, and improve overall herd performance.

Milk Quality

"Higher Milk Volume Doesn't Always Mean Higher Profit"

Milk buyers don't just pay for litres—they pay for quality. Low milk fat and Solids-Not-Fat (SNF) reduce milk value and farmer income. Optimizing nutrition helps improve milk composition, enhance product quality, and maximize returns from every litre produced.

Milk Yield & Feed Intake

"Reduced Feed Intake Leads to Reduced Profitability"

Milk production begins with consistent feed intake. When Dry Matter Intake (DMI) declines, animals receive fewer nutrients for maintenance and milk production, resulting in lower yield and weight loss. Improving feed intake and nutrient utilization sustains higher milk production while supporting animal health.

Immune Support

"Prevention Is Always More Profitable Than Treatment"

Poor immunity increases infection risks, treatment costs, production losses, and premature culling. Strengthening the animal's natural defence system improves resilience against disease challenges while supporting healthier, longer-lasting, and more productive herds.
