Why Jahaaj

More Than Manufacturing. We Build Products Together.

At Jahaaj Healthcare, we go beyond conventional contract manufacturing. As a Contract Development & Manufacturing Organization (CDMO) for animal health and nutrition, we work alongside you from idea and formulation to trials, manufacturing, packaging, and commercialization. With collaborative development, formulation expertise, flexible product formats, quality-driven processes, and proactive support, we help turn opportunities into manufacturable, scalable, and market-ready products.

You bring the opportunity. We help build what comes next.

Partner With Jahaaj →
1

Product Ideas: We Don’t Just Manufacture Products. We Help Create Them.

Jahaaj brings product-development thinking to the manufacturing relationship—from concept stage through commercialization.

💡 New Product Opportunities

Helping identify market gaps, evaluate regional needs, and build viable product concepts.

🔬 Formulation Innovation

New ideas, product differentiation, emerging dosage forms, and optimized bio-availability.

🚀 End-to-End Support

Seamless coordination across recipe scoping, trial piloting, and commercial manufacturing.

2

FAMI-QS Certified: Built Around International Feed-Safety Expectations

Jahaaj Healthcare is a FAMI-QS certified manufacturing facility, supporting a structured approach to feed safety and quality management—designed to meet the rigorous quality expectations required for professional and international animal nutrition businesses.

🛡️ Controlled Processes

Strict raw-material quality controls, in-line process monitoring, and complete lot traceability.

📋 Defined Quality Systems

Standardized manufacturing operating procedures (SOPs) and comprehensive validation records.

🔍 Quality Oversight

Full documentation, analytical Certificates of Analysis (CoA), and audit readiness throughout.

3

Product Journey: One Partner Across the Complete Lifecycle

From the first idea to the finished commercial product, Jahaaj supports multiple stages of development with a single dedicated Point of Contact (POC)—fewer handovers, easier coordination, and a more integrated path from idea to market success.

Phase 1

Idea & Concept

Define vision & assess feasibility.

Phase 2

Formulation & R&D

Develop formula & laboratory testing.

Phase 3

Trial & Optimization

Scale up, test and refine processes.

Phase 4

Scale & Launch

Produce at scale and dispatch.

4

In-House Veterinary R&D & Customized Formulations

Formulation expertise supported by a veterinary doctor with 40+ years of formulation experience—contributing to ingredient selection, product optimization, dosage-form decisions, and manufacturing feasibility.

Customized Formulations are tailored around specific requirements—not limited to a fixed catalogue:

  • Ingredient profile, inclusion levels, and target species (Poultry, Ruminants, Pets, Swine, Aqua, Equine)
  • Nutritional, functional, metabolic, or therapeutic objectives
  • Product format, physical characteristics, palatability, and cost targets
  • Custom primary and secondary packaging requirements
5

The Triangular Balance of a Reliable Manufacturing Partnership

At Jahaaj, we believe a strong manufacturing partnership is built on three equally important, interdependent pillars:

1. Quality

Controlled processes, defined specifications, consistent batch quality, and complete analytical traceability.

2. Transparency

Raw-material traceability, supplier transparency, live production updates, open communication, and full documentation.

3. On-Time Availability

Production planning, supply continuity, timely manufacturing, and responsive dispatch coordination.
