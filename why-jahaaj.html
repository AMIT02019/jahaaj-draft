03 • Product Journey

One Partner Across the Complete Product Journey

From the first idea to the finished commercial product, Jahaaj supports multiple stages of development with a dedicated Point of Contact (POC) — fewer handovers, easier coordination, and a more integrated path from idea to commercialization.

PHASE 01

Idea & Concept

Define vision and feasibility. Market analysis, target species identification, active ingredient selection, and viability.

PHASE 02

Formulation & R&D

Develop formulas and lab testing. Bioavailability optimization, excipient balance, and pilot batch prototyping.

PHASE 03

Trial & Optimization

Scale-up trials and refine process. Stability testing, palatability checks, dosage calibration, and barrier validation.

PHASE 04

Manufacturing & Commercialization

Produce at scale and launch. Full commercial execution, regulatory documentation, automated packaging, and market delivery.
