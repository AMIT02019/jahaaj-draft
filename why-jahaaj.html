Why Jahaaj Healthcare

Choosing the right manufacturing partner is not just about capacity it's about trust, consistency, and long-term reliability. At Jahaaj Healthcare Pvt. Ltd., we are built to support animal health brands at every stage from conceptualising the product to scaling output, committed to bringing scientifically-engineered solutions and revolutionising animal husbandry in India.

Partner With Us

We don't work as vendors. We work as manufacturing partners to take your brand from zero to one.

1

Quality That Is Built into Every Step

At Jahaaj, quality isn't an afterthought it's a guarantee woven into our ethos.

  • FAMI-QS certified manufacturing systems
  • Rigorous raw-material testing from audited vendors only
  • In-process checks and finished product verification
  • Strong QC, QA, and microbiology oversight
  • HACCP-based safety, VACCP, and TACCP systems in place
2

Designed for Private Label & Contract Manufacturing

Jahaaj is structured specifically to support private label & contract manufacturing.

  • Customized formulations as per market and customer needs
  • Flexible batch sizes with scalable production capability
  • Support for packaging, artwork, and label revisions
  • Smooth coordination between commercial, production, and quality teams
3

Transparent, Partner-First Approach

We believe strong partnerships are built on visibility and trust.

  • Transparent documentation and process flow
  • Live production updates when required
  • Clear communication with commercial and technical alignment
  • Active involvement from top management
4

Ethical Business Practices

Ethics are non-negotiable for us.

  • Timely and fair vendor payments
  • Responsible procurement practices
  • Continuous employee training on safety and compliance
  • Regular policy reviews and system improvements
5

Strong Infrastructure & Operational Discipline

Our 12,000 sq. ft. facility at Rabale MIDC, Navi Mumbai handles diversified animal health products efficiently.

  • Powder blending and sifting with 1,200 TPA capacity
  • Bolus manufacturing (compression) with 120 TPA capacity
  • Semi-automatic repacking for pet and livestock products
6

People-Driven, System-Backed Manufacturing

At Jahaaj, change is driven by our people with intricate systems that push them to deliver.

  • Experienced leadership directly involved in operations
  • Skilled production and quality teams
  • Cross-functional coordination between sales, QC, and manufacturing
  • Dedicated support for trials, documentation, and audits
7

Built for Long-Term Growth

Our goal is simple: your growth, without manufacturing stress. We help partners:

  • Reduce operational risks with compliant systems
  • Maintain consistent product quality at every batch
  • Scale confidently into new markets
  • Build brand credibility with FAMI-QS certified manufacturing
