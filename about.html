Capacity & Infrastructure

Manufacturing Excellence & Controlled Quality

Equipped with automated manufacturing lines, high-precision micro-dosing systems, and multi-stage testing for peak batch consistency.

3,000 Kg / Day

Powder Manufacturing

Controlled blending and packaging environment engineered for homogenous dispersion, anti-caking integrity, and precise micronutrient distribution.

  • Double-cone and ribbon blenders
  • Nitrogen-purged multi-layer foil packaging
  • Sachet, jar, and bulk bag packaging
30,000 Bolus / Day

Bolus & Tablet Manufacturing

Heavy-tonnage rotary presses with tight tolerance controls for hardness, disintegration time, and active ingredient stability in veterinary boluses.

  • High-speed rotary bolus compression
  • Blister pack and strip pack options
  • Fast-disintegrating and sustained-release
3,000 Litres / Day

Liquid Manufacturing

Stainless steel SS-316 reactor vessels with high-shear emulsification, automated multi-head filling, and induction sealing lines.

  • SS-316 high-shear homogenization
  • Automated filling from 100ml to 20L
  • Induction cap sealing & tamper-proof caps
