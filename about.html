Who We Are

At Jahaaj Healthcare Pvt. Ltd., we are more than manufacturers we are long-term partners in animal health and nutrition. Founded in 2017, Jahaaj was built with one clear purpose: to help animal health brands launch, scale, and succeed with science-backed, safe, and ethically manufactured nutritional supplements. Recognised as a FAMI-QS certified company in 2023, we specialize in private label & contract manufacturing of animal health supplements, catering to poultry, livestock, pets, aqua & swine. From innovative feed premixes and probiotics to toxin binders and pet nutrition products, Jahaaj supports brands across India and beyond with dependable quality and transparent processes.

Our Services

Private Label Manufacturing: Formula customization, packaging, labelling, branding & regulatory guidance. Contract Manufacturing: Responsible scaling of your formulation using certified infrastructure & strict QC.

Our Manufacturing Strength: Operating from a 12,000 sq. ft. facility at Rabale MIDC, Navi Mumbai.

Modern infrastructure handling:

  • Powder blending & premix manufacturing (Capacity: 1200 TPA)
  • Bolus compression manufacturing (Capacity: 120 TPA)
  • Semi-automatic repacking for pet and livestock products
  • Scalable batch sizes with consistent quality output & fast repeat execution.
