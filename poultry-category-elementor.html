Feed Conversion Ratio (FCR)

“When Feed Costs Increase, Profits Shrink”

Feed is the single largest expense in poultry production, accounting for 60-80% of total production costs. Even a small increase in FCR can translate into tonnes of additional feed consumption over a production cycle, significantly reducing profit margins. Poor nutrient utilization, digestive inefficiencies, stress, and suboptimal gut health often prevent birds from converting feed into body weight efficiently. Our nutritional solutions are designed to improve feed utilization, helping birds grow efficiently while reducing feed wastage and production costs.

Gut Health

“A Weak Gut Costs More Than You Think”

Not every production loss begins with disease. When gut health is compromised, birds absorb fewer nutrients from the same amount of feed. This often leads to slower growth, inconsistent flock uniformity, reduced immunity, increased medication costs, and poorer overall performance. Maintaining a healthy digestive system allows birds to make better use of every gram of feed while building stronger natural defences.

Formulated Solution: Digestive & Gut Health Probiotic Complex (Powder)

Loose Droppings & Wet Litter

“Wet Litter Doesn't Just Affect the Floor-It Affects Your Bottom Line”

Wet litter is often treated as a management issue, but its impact goes much further. Excess moisture increases ammonia production, damages footpads, reduces bird comfort, promotes bacterial growth, and raises the risk of myiasis and other respiratory diseases. It also increases labour requirements and litter replacement costs. Supporting digestive efficiency helps produce firmer droppings, healthier litter, and a more productive growing environment.

Mycotoxin Management

“The Most Expensive Feed Problem Is the One You Can't See”

Feed can look perfectly normal while silently reducing flock performance. Mycotoxins contaminate up to 60% of crops worldwide each year and can significantly reduce feed efficiency, suppress immunity, damage the liver, impair reproduction, and increase mortality-all without obvious early warning signs. Protecting birds from hidden feed contaminants helps preserve performance, improve flock health, and reduce avoidable economic losses.

Egg Production

“Every Egg Lost Is Revenue You Never Recover”

A small decline in laying percentage may seem insignificant-but across thousands of birds, it quickly becomes a substantial financial loss. Nutritional deficiencies, oxidative stress, poor gut health, and metabolic challenges can all reduce laying persistence and flock consistency. Supporting reproductive health helps maintain egg production, improve flock uniformity, and maximize profitability throughout the laying cycle.

Every infertile egg represents lost production potential. Poor semen quality and reduced fertility decrease hatchability, increase production costs, and lower the number of healthy chicks entering the production cycle. Providing targeted nutritional support helps maintain breeder performance and improve reproductive efficiency.

Kidney Health & Gout

“Hidden Kidney Stress Can Become a Costly Problem”

Kidney damage often develops gradually before visible clinical signs appear. As kidney function declines, birds become more susceptible to gout, dehydration, reduced growth, and increased mortality-especially during periods of nutritional or environmental stress. Supporting kidney health helps maintain productivity while reducing losses associated with metabolic disorders.

Early Chick Nutrition

“Build Strong Birds from Day One”

A chick only gets one chance to start well. Research consistently shows that early feed intake and intestinal development influence lifetime growth, feed efficiency, and immune competence. A poor start often results in reduced body weight that becomes difficult to recover later. Providing targeted nutritional support during the first week helps build stronger, healthier birds capable of reaching their full genetic potential.

Ammonia Management

“You Can't Improve Performance Without Improving the Environment”

Poor air quality quietly reduces productivity every single day. High ammonia levels damage the respiratory tract, reduce feed intake, increase stress, compromise immunity, and make birds more susceptible to disease outbreaks. Creating a cleaner environment supports healthier birds, better welfare, and improved production efficiency.

Fly Management

“Small Pests. Big Production Losses.”

Flies are more than a nuisance-they're efficient carriers of pathogens. Heavy fly populations increase disease transmission, compromise biosecurity, stress birds, and create sanitation issues that affect farm operations and customer confidence. Effective fly management contributes to healthier flocks and cleaner production facilities.
