Class 10,000 Micro Cleanroom Suite
Sterility & Hygiene

Zero-Tolerance Pathogen Testing Architecture

Animal feed and veterinary supplements require uncompromising microbiological control to prevent flock and herd mortality. Our dedicated Class 10,000 cleanroom suite isolates testing environments from all factory manufacturing zones.

  • Specific Pathogen Absence: Verification of complete absence of Salmonella species (in 25g sample) and Escherichia coli (in 1g sample).
  • Yeast & Mould Enumeration: Stringent limits (<100 CFU/g) to prevent mycotoxin elaboration during feed storage.
  • Laminar Airflow Protection: Class 100 sterile working surfaces under vertical HEPA laminar airflow workstations.
Precision Digital Incubation Suite
Probiotic Viability

Live Probiotic CFU Enumeration & Stability

For private label brands marketing multi-strain probiotic powders and water-soluble complexes, we provide verified Colony Forming Unit (CFU) counts that guarantee active viability across the shelf life.

  • Multi-Strain Enumeration: Differentiating and counting Bacillus subtilis, Lactobacillus acidophilus, and live Saccharomyces cerevisiae cultures.
  • Pelleting Heat Survival Assays: Simulating commercial feed conditioning temperatures to confirm spore coat survival.
  • Environmental Monitoring: Routine air settling plates and equipment surface contact swabbing to audit cleanroom sterility.
Standard Testing Protocols

Microbiological Assays & Testing Criteria

Every raw material shipment and finished batch is evaluated according to global veterinary hygiene regulations.

Salmonella Detection Assay

Pre-enrichment, selective broth culture, and chromogenic agar plating verifying absolute absence of Salmonella in 25 grams of sample.

Standard: Absent in 25g

Escherichia coli Enumeration

MacConkey broth fermentation and selective EMB agar culture guaranteeing zero fecal coliform contamination in finished powders.

Standard: Absent in 1g

Probiotic CFU Viability

Serial dilution and pour-plate enumeration on MRS and Nutrient agar confirming label claim potency ($1 \times 10^9$ to $1 \times 10^{11}\text{ CFU/kg}$).

Guaranteed Potency Protocol

Total Aerobic Microbial Count (TAMC)

Standard plate count agar evaluating total viable bioburden, ensuring product falls well below international feed safety limits.

Limit: < 10,000 CFU/g

Total Yeast & Mould Count (TYMC)

Sabouraud Dextrose Agar incubation at 25°C for 5 days screening against fungal mycelia and spore proliferation.

Limit: < 100 CFU/g

Environmental Cleanroom Swabs

Weekly microbial air monitoring and equipment contact plates auditing cleanroom hygiene, HVAC filtration, and personnel gowning.
