About Swine Health Solutions

Pig farm profitability comes down to two numbers: average daily gain and scour-free days. Our swine range is built around exactly those two levers a botanical growth promoter that lifts weight gain, and a probiotic-prebiotic blend that controls diarrhea and keeps gut health steady through weaning.

Both formulations are powder-based, private-label ready, and can be combined into a single multi-benefit product through our custom formulation builder below.
