The full system, step by step

1

Ingredient Quality Management

Every great product begins with the right raw materials. Since they account for a major portion of manufacturing cost and quality variation, we treat ingredient control as the first quality gate.

  • Defined physical and sensory checks (appearance, odor, texture)
  • Verification of supplier specifications
  • Controlled sampling procedures during material receipt via sampling booth only
  • Laboratory evaluation using validated analytical methods
  • Strong documentation for acceptance, rejection, and traceability

We don't chase the cheapest ingredients we choose the most reliable ones. When inputs are consistent and predictable, performance never wavers.

2

Process Control: Quality Inside the Plant

Once ingredients enter the facility, quality depends on how processes are executed and controlled.

People

  • Trained operators who understand what they do and why it matters
  • Clear accountability across production, IPQC, and supervision
  • Reinforcement of quality responsibility across all levels

Machinery

  • Equipment selected and operated within designed capacity
  • Preventive maintenance schedules
  • Regular inspection of wear points, discharge points, sieves, mixers, and conveyors
  • Calibration and verification of weighing balances, meters

Procedures

  • Clear communication
  • Identification and labeling
  • Batch traceability
  • Verification through sampling
  • Record integrity and accessibility
3

Batch Accuracy & Mixing Validation

Batching and mixing are among the most critical quality steps in manufacturing.

  • Controlled batch sequencing
  • Validation of batch weights
  • Routine inspection of mixers and components
  • Defined mixing times and validation checks
  • Monitoring for cross-contamination and residue carryover

Consistency at this stage ensures uniform nutrient distribution and product reliability.

4

Equipment Cleanliness & Safety Controls

Quality is also about preventing contamination before it starts.

  • Scheduled inspection and cleaning of storage bins
  • Equipment cleanliness verification
  • Magnet checks and metal contamination control
  • Safety protocols for confined space access
  • Lockout and isolation procedures during maintenance

This protects both product integrity and employee safety.

5

Finished Product Quality Assurance

Finished products serve as the final report card of our systems. Our QC/QA team performs:

  • Routine finished product sampling
  • In-process and post-production checks
  • Documentation of test results
  • Investigation and root-cause analysis in case of deviations

Defined corrective actions are taken immediately to prevent recurrence.

6

Microbiological & Safety Control

Product safety is non-negotiable. Our microbiological controls focus on:

  • Monitoring potential pathogenic risks
  • Preventing moisture-related spoilage
  • Verifying hygiene and sanitation effectiveness
  • Maintaining records for regulatory and client audits

This layer protects animals, farmers, and supply chains.

7

Continuous Improvement & Audit Readiness

Quality control does not stop with testing it improves through evaluation.

  • Continuous review of records and trends
  • Support for internal, third-party, and client audits
  • SOP upgrades based on findings and learnings
  • Cross-department communication on quality outcomes

Even when issues arise, our focus remains on learning, correcting, and strengthening systems.
