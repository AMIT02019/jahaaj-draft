Quality Journey

From First Check to Final Release

A structured 7-stage quality gate ensuring absolute feed safety and consistency.

01

Raw Materials

Every ingredient starts with defined quality checks and verified specifications.

02

Process Control

Trained teams, calibrated equipment, and documented procedures keep production consistent.

03

Batch & Mixing

Validated weights, controlled sequencing, and mixing checks ensure complete uniformity.

04

Safety & Cleanliness

Cleaning protocols, contamination controls, and equipment safety protect product integrity.

05

Finished Product

Every batch undergoes sampling, analytical testing, and documented QA checks before release.

06

Microbiological Control

Safety-focused monitoring helps prevent contamination and protect long-term product shelf life.

07

Continuous Improvement

Audits, trend reviews, and corrective actions continuously strengthen our manufacturing systems.
