Research Architecture

The 4-Stage Formulation Discovery Lifecycle

Every Jahaaj formulation originates from empirical farm-level biology and advances through rigorous analytical assays before commercial scale-up.

Stage 01

Target Identification

Mapping species-specific pathology, gut dysbiosis, thermal distress markers, and trace mineral deficiencies observed in intensive commercial herds and flocks.

Key Output: Biological Challenge Dossier
Stage 02

Active Synergy Design

Selecting multi-strain probiotics, chelated minerals, organic acids, and phytogenic extracts with synergistic bioavailability and anti-microbial potency.

Key Output: Synergistic Formula Architecture
Stage 03

Stability & Bioavailability

Engineering thermo-tolerant micro-encapsulation and flowability matrixes to withstand high-shear feed pelleting temperatures (up to 85°C) without loss of active titers.

Key Output: 24-Month Stability Verification
Stage 04

Pilot Scale Validation

Conducting 50 kg to 500 kg trial pilot batches in our licensed cleanroom suites, validating dissolution rates, homogeneity indices (CV < 5%), and sensory palatability.

Key Output: Commercial CDMO Tech Transfer

Problem-Specific R&D

Formulation Discoveries by Health Challenge

We categorize active nutritional research directly around real-world commercial production bottlenecks.

Gut Dysbiosis & FCR

Multi-strain spore probiotics and bacteriocin-producing fermentates that outcompete pathogenic enterobacteria in broilers and piglets.

Rumen Acidosis & Milk Fat

Bypassed buffer complexes and live rumen yeast cultures (Saccharomyces cerevisiae) designed to stabilize pH and maximize dry matter intake.

Heat Stress & Electrolytes

Fast-dissolving effervescent and powder formulations replenishing potassium, sodium bicarbonate, and organic betaine during high-temperature periods.

Broad-Spectrum Mycotoxin Binding

Modified aluminosilicates, activated yeast cell wall beta-glucans, and enzymatic detoxifiers targeting Aflatoxin, T-2, and Ochratoxin.

Eggshell & Skeletal Calcification

Bioavailable organic calcium pidolate, vitamin D3 metabolites, and magnesium chelates that eliminate thin-shell and breakage losses.

Aquaculture Pond Biosecurity

Microbial pond bioremediators and gut colonizers reducing toxic ammonia ($NH_3$) and nitrite ($NO_2^-$) in intensive shrimp and fish ponds.
